Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Turkish version: Gizlilik politikası (prevails in case of conflict)

Summary

  • You can play the daily game without an account; we identify you only by a random ID.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of data.
  • Delete your account any time in the app: Settings → Delete account.

Data we collect

  • Random user ID (anonymous session); game data (guesses, scores, streak, 1v1 matches and rating, challenge codes).
  • Optional nickname shown on leaderboards (can be hidden).
  • Email and account ID only if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Pseudonymous usage events, device model, OS and app version for diagnostics and product improvement. No name, email or location is attached.
  • Purchase record for Pro (payments are processed by Apple/Google; we never see card details).
  • Photo reports you submit; waitlist email if you join it on our website; support emails.

We do not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo library access. The pin you drop on the map is a game guess, not your location.

Processors

Supabase (database, auth, storage — Frankfurt, EU), PostHog (analytics — region to be confirmed), RevenueCat (purchase validation — USA), Expo (push delivery and app updates — USA), Apple and Google (stores, payments, optional sign-in).

Retention

Game data: while your account exists; deleted within 30 days after account deletion. Inactive anonymous accounts: deleted after 24 months. Analytics: max 24 months. Waitlist: deleted within 6 months after launch. Support emails: 2 years.

Your rights

Access, rectification, deletion, objection and portability. Contact destek@maziturkiye.com with the ID shown at the bottom of the Settings screen. We reply within 30 days.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13.
